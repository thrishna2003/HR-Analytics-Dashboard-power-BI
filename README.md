# HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses employee attrition and key HR metrics for 271 employees.

## Key Metrics
- Total employees: 271
- Total attrition: 73
- Attrition rate: 26.94%
- Active employees: 198
- Average age: 35
- Average monthly pay: 5,812

## What the dashboard shows
- Employees by age group and gender
- Attrition by age
- Attrition by department
- Attrition rate by job role
- Job satisfaction rating by job role
- Attrition by education field
- Slicers for gender, marital status, business travel and education level

## Key Insights
- Attrition is highest among Sales Representatives (42.11%) and Laboratory Technicians (41.38%).
- Most employees are in the 25-34 and 35-44 age groups.
- Life Sciences is the education field with the most attrition (27 employees).

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modelling, DAX measures, visuals)
- Excel / CSV (dataset)
